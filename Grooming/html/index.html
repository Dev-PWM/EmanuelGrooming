What Emanuel Pet Grooming Really Is

We are not a rushed, loud, conveyor-belt pet factory. We are a family-owned pet care sanctuary built on deep love, patience, and gentle handling.

As owner and founder of Emanuel Pet Grooming, we understand that you and your pet need to feel completely comfortable and safe. That is why our grooming is based in a calm, ultra-clean home setting with a relaxed atmosphere that caters to all your dog and cat's individual needs.

Your pet will be treated as one of our own. While here, they have plenty of time to rest, stretch their legs in sanitized resting areas, and enjoy fresh filtered water so their experience is joyful, not stressful.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything pet parents want to know before their first visit to Emanuel Pet Grooming.

How is Emanuel Pet Grooming different from retail pet stores?
+
We operate in a calm, sanitary home-style sanctuary without the chaos, deafening barking, and high turnover of big box retail stores. We provide gentle, 1-on-1 personalized attention with low noise and regular comfort breaks, ensuring your pet feels safe and loved.
Do you groom both cats and dogs?
+
Yes! We groom dogs of all breeds and sizes, as well as cats. For our feline guests, we maintain an especially quiet atmosphere with soft handling, gentle sponge baths or waterless foams, deshedding, sanitary clips, or full lion cuts.
What if my pet is nervous, aggressive, or has anxiety?
+
Patience is our specialty. We never force a terrified pet through a scary procedure. We take time to build trust, use positive reinforcement, and take soothing breaks whenever needed. Please let us know when booking so we can schedule ample time.
How long does a grooming appointment take?
+
Most appointments take between 1.5 to 2.5 hours depending on your pet's size, coat condition, and required styling. We will text or call you 15-20 minutes before your pet is ready for pickup!
What vaccines are required before grooming?
+
For the health and safety of all our furry guests, up-to-date Rabies vaccination is required for all dogs and cats. Bordetella (kennel cough) and DHPP are strongly recommended for dogs.
