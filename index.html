The studio

We’re not a rushed, noisy, conveyor-belt pet factory. We’re a calm grooming studio where every dog and cat gets patience, clean tools and gentle hands — and leaves feeling loved.

Your pet is treated like one of our own. They get time to rest, stretch their legs and drink fresh filtered water, so the whole day feels joyful instead of stressful.

  • Never rushed

    We read your pet’s body language and take soothing breaks whenever they need one.

  • Cats & dogs welcome

    A gentle, dedicated approach for felines and canines of every size.

  • Spotless tools

    Tables, shears, tubs and rest areas are disinfected between visits.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How is Emanuel Pet Grooming different from a big-box pet store?

We’re a calm, one-on-one studio, with no wall of barking and no assembly line. Your pet gets individual attention, a relaxed pace and comfort breaks, so the whole visit feels safe.

Do you groom both cats and dogs?

Yes, we groom both dogs and cats. For our feline guests we keep things especially calm and quiet.

What if my pet is nervous or has anxiety?

Patience is our specialty. We never force a frightened pet through something scary. We build trust, use positive reinforcement and take soothing breaks. Tell us when you book so we can set aside extra time.

How long does a grooming appointment take?

It depends on your pet’s size, coat condition and package. We text or call you when your pet is ready for pickup.

What vaccines are required before grooming?

To keep every furry guest safe, an up-to-date rabies vaccination is required for all dogs and cats. Bordetella (kennel cough) and DHPP are strongly recommended for dogs.
